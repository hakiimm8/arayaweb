WHAT WE DO

Typical marine project scopes.

01

Fire alarm, general alarm & AMS

Fire detection, general alarm and alarm-monitoring systems: fault finding, replacement of panels, detectors and displays, and full retrofit.

02

Engine safety devices

Safety, alarm and shutdown systems for main and auxiliary engines, including trip circuits, sensors and Sinpol-D safety devices.

03

Engine overhaul & control

Mechanical overhaul alongside engine monitoring, protection and control, including throttle and governor systems.

04

Generator synchronisation & PMS

Synchronisation, automatic start/stop, load sharing and power management for ship generators and switchboards.

05

Manoeuvring, CPP & bow thrusters

Bridge-to-engine manoeuvring systems, controllable-pitch propellers and bow thrusters, including control panels and feedback.

06

Navigation & bridge equipment

Autopilot, gyro compass, steering systems and public address installations on the bridge and throughout the vessel.

07

Electrical panels, PLC & SCADA

Control and distribution panels, PLC logic, HMI operator screens and SCADA monitoring for onboard systems.

08

Tank level & cargo monitoring

Water-level, tank and cargo monitoring: sensors, signal conditioning, alarm thresholds and displays.

09

Deck cranes & deck machinery

Mechanical, electrical and control work on deck cranes, winches and deck machinery, from fault finding to overhaul.

10

AC & refrigeration systems

Air-conditioning and refrigeration plant on board, including electrical control, protection and fault diagnosis.

11

Retrofit of obsolete systems

Replacing obsolete or end-of-sale control equipment while keeping the machinery that still works, planned around your docking window.

12

Hydrodynamic test facilities

Specialist design and engineering for marine research facilities, including ocean basins, towing tanks and hydrodynamics laboratories.
