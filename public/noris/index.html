Inside the Noris portfolio.

01

Sensors & measuring points

Speed · Temperature · Pressure

Noris sensors provide measurement inputs for machinery and marine applications. Manufacturer examples include FAH13/FAJ13 speed sensors and TA.81/TA.82 temperature sensors.

Applications
Engine and gearbox measurement, water or process monitoring points where the sensor specification is suitable.
Selection details
Measuring range, medium, mounting, connector, output signal, and environmental conditions.
Manufacturer details
02

Instruments & analogue indicators

noriMeter · NIR3 / NIQ3 · NIQ31

Noris analogue instruments display values such as speed, temperature, pressure, and position. The portfolio includes stepper-motor and moving-coil designs, with round or square housings.

Applications
Local indication, control consoles, and instrument-panel replacement or retrofit.
Selection details
Input signal, scale, cut-out dimensions, illumination, and the required indication.
Manufacturer details
03

Signal processing

Measuring transducers · Limit switches

Signal-conditioning devices connect sensors to the wider measuring system. Manufacturer examples include VF5 frequency transducers and VP5/VPT5 temperature transducers.

Applications
Converting measurement signals and providing limit signals to a monitoring or control system.
Selection details
Input and output signals, supply voltage, isolation requirements, and switching thresholds.
Manufacturer details
04

Marine alarm, monitoring & control

noriMos 4 · noriMos 3500

The noriMos portfolio combines onboard data acquisition, alarm indication, visualisation, and control. Its system architectures are selected to suit the vessel and integration requirements.

Applications
Engine-room AMS, vessel monitoring, and automation retrofit discussions.
Selection details
Signal list, operating stations, existing interfaces, redundancy needs, and vessel requirements.
Manufacturer details
