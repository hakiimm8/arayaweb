# Preview audit — performance, UI design and content

Audited 9 October 2026 against content release `f19cc41` (published preview) and the current production site.

- Preview: https://hakiimm8.github.io/arayaweb/
- Production: https://www.arayainternusa.co.id/ (302 → `/araya/`)

Method: Lighthouse 12.8.2 (single lab run per page, default mobile and desktop presets, headless Chromium from a cloud container), Playwright screenshots at 1440×900 and 390×844, and source review of `public/` and `tools/`. Lab scores vary between runs. Preview runs on GitHub Pages and production runs on WordPress hosting, so the comparison shows the cost of each page's front end. It does not compare the hosts. Raw reports were not committed because each is about 1 MB.

## Summary

The preview is a large improvement on production in every area measured. Main remaining gaps:

1. **Mobile layout shift (CLS 0.262)** on every page, caused by the no-JavaScript navigation fallback. One-line fix.
2. **Trust content from the live site was dropped.** It had client logos, the 6-month work guarantee, technical backup, team, mission/vision and business hours.
3. **The contact path depends on `mailto:`.** WhatsApp, the main business channel in Indonesia, appears only in the footer.
4. **Homepage priorities.** The first CTA promotes a distributor brand ("Explore Noris"), not Araya's own services or a contact action.
5. **English only.** There is no Bahasa Indonesia version for the local market.

| Medium | No responsive images. ## 2. UI design

The preview keeps the production identity (orange/charcoal/white, Space Grotesk, original logo) and is much cleaner. It has a clear hierarchy, consistent section headings, real photography and no cookie overlay covering the hero. No horizontal overflow and no console errors were found at 1440px or 390px.

| Priority | Finding | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **High** | Hero has three equal-weight CTAs, and the primary one is "Explore Noris". On mobile they stack into three full-height buttons that fill the first screen. | Primary: "Discuss your project" (WhatsApp). Secondary: "Our services". Move Noris/ComAp to the brand section, which already links them. |
| Medium | Text below 12px: brand-panel labels "NORIS GROUP GmbH" / "ENGINE & POWER CONTROL" (10px), mobile utility bar and copyright (10px), hero index (9px). | Minimum 12px; 13–14px for contact details. |
| Medium | Colour-contrast failures. White on `#ee5c03` in the "2009" badge is 3.4:1. `.eyebrow` and `#cb4800` index numbers on `#f6f6f6` are 4.35:1. | Use `--orange-readable` (#cb4800) as the badge background. Darken orange text on the muted background to about `#b84100`. |
| Medium | Logo is distorted by CSS (`aspect-ratio:238/52` on a 291×52 file), and Lighthouse flags it on every page. The notes say the source upload is stretched. | Fix the asset, not the display. Re-export the original artwork at true proportions so the `<img>` dimensions match, with no redrawing. |
| Medium | Hero index "01 / MARINE & AUTOMATION" is left over from a slider, but there is no slider. | Remove it, or show a real fact ("Since 2009 · 1,500+ projects"). |
| Medium | Photos repeat on the homepage. `control-room.webp` and `industrial.webp` each appear twice (Services and Projects), with different alt text. | Use distinct project photos in "Our Projects". Production has more on-site photos. |
| Low | Hero photo is flat and overcast, so the shaded overlay makes it read grey. | Production's Noris control-console photo is more on-message, or grade the ship photo warmer and brighter. |

| Low | ComAp diagram triggers: the visible "Enlarge" text is not in the `aria-label`, a WCAG 2.5.3 label-in-name failure. The dialog source link has no `href` until opened. | Start each `aria-label` with the visible text. Give the source link a default `href`. |
| Low | Favicon is only a 1.9 KB PNG. | Add `apple-touch-icon` (180px) and a 32px/SVG icon. |

## 3. Content

### What is better than production

Production still carries theme-demo content that the preview removes:

- "Love to build your dream", "Best Construction Experts" and "We Provide Our Services All Over The World".
- Animated counters stuck at "0+ Years" and "0k+ Projects".
- No `<h1>`, and 33 images without alt text.
- A demo-theme UAE phone link (`tel: +971 551 579 261`) and `support.industrie@gmail.com` in the markup.

The preview has descriptive titles and meta descriptions, one H1 per page, alt text on every image, and dedicated, sourced Noris and ComAp guides. Production had none of these.

### Gaps and recommendations

| Priority | Finding | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **High** | **Trust signals from production are missing.** Production shows client logos (PELNI, Pelindo, Pertamina, TNI, PT Armada Cakrawala Esa and others), the 6-month work guarantee, technical backup from HIT / Noris / ComAp, the leadership team with years of experience, mission/vision and "Mon–Sat 08.00–17.00". The preview's only proof point is "1,500+ projects". | Add a "Trusted by" logo row (with client permission), a guarantee/support strip in place of the generic feature strip, and business hours in the header/footer. Optionally add a short leadership section. |
| **High** | **"Our Projects" has no projects.** It shows scope lists and coverage regions, but no named or dated work. | Add 3–6 short case studies: vessel or plant type, region, scope, brand used, year, one photo. Anonymise the client if needed. Production already has dated job photos. |
| **High** | **Contact relies on `mailto:`.** "Discuss your project" fails without a configured mail client, common on mobile and office PCs. WhatsApp appears only in the footer. | Make WhatsApp the main CTA with a pre-filled message (`wa.me/6281326396262?text=…`), add a floating WhatsApp button on mobile, and add a short enquiry form on production WordPress. |
| **High** | **English only** for a domestic B2B audience. Production already mixes in Indonesian (the motto and "Tenaga Ahli"). | Add a Bahasa Indonesia version, at least for home, services and the contact CTA, with `hreflang`. Many local searches, such as "distributor ComAp Indonesia" or "sensor Noris kapal", will be in Indonesian. |
| Medium | Feature strip copy is generic ("Start with your system and application requirements", "Local conversation"). | Replace with concrete differentiators: guarantee, response time, manufacturer backup, Sidoarjo base with nationwide coverage. |
| Medium | Brand pages read like internal compliance notes in places. Examples: "A controller alone does not establish a datacentre Tier rating." and "It is not presented here as an available Indonesian tariff service; confirm regional support before specifying it." | Keep these scope boundaries in the docs. On the page, write one positive line, such as "Araya confirms the right model and scope for your project", and cut the negative caveats. |
| Medium | ComAp page is long (3,365 words, 19 applications, 13 diagrams). Noris has 1,479 words. The home page has about 540. | Keep the application index and diagrams. Collapse the product catalogue or split it into `/comap/marine/`, `/comap/power-generation/` and similar. Each sub-page then targets its own search terms. |
| Medium | Facts are uneven. Production says the director has 20 years of experience and calls Araya a "Certified and Authorized Partner". The preview deliberately avoids authorization claims. | Get the owner to confirm the distributor or authorization status in writing. Once confirmed, state it plainly: it is the strongest sales message for both brand pages. |
| Low | Titles over 60 characters will be truncated in Google: ComAp 81, Noris 74, industrial 70, marine 67. | For example "ComAp Distributor Indonesia – Engine & Power Control \| Araya" (≤60). |
| Low | Organization schema has no address, opening hours or `sameAs`. | Use `LocalBusiness` with the Sidoarjo address, geo, hours and social profiles at production launch. Add `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` then too. |
| Low | The address has no map link. | Link the footer address to Google Maps. |

## Suggested order of work

1. Inline the `.js` class to fix CLS. Fix the badge and eyebrow contrast, raise the minimum font size, and fix the ComAp `aria-label`s. All small CSS/HTML edits in `tools/build.py` / `site.css`.
2. Make WhatsApp the primary CTA. Rework the hero CTAs and remove the hero index.
3. Restore trust content after owner confirmation: client logos, guarantee, hours, team.
4. Add `srcset` image variants and a JPEG `og:image`.
5. Gather case studies. Plan the Bahasa Indonesia version and ComAp sub-pages.
6. At production migration: long cache headers, `LocalBusiness` schema, sitemap/robots. Remove `noindex`.
